"Pick an adventure" / "Tap a card to see its games."
About: "BAYANIHanda Game Library turns everyday safety lessons into short, playable adventures for children. The games are grouped around the four parts of disaster risk reduction and management: preventing and reducing hazards, preparing before a disaster, responding when one strikes, and recovering afterward." More: "The concepts behind each pillar are turned into short, hands-on games so learning about disasters becomes fun instead of frightening. The playful approach draws young learners in and helps the lessons stick. Every game runs fully offline on a phone or tablet, keeps no personal data, and speaks its tips aloud so younger and older children can play side by side." "Finish a game with flying colors and you earn a badge for your Champion ID. Together the games build the calm, ready habits that help a child act well in the first minutes of an emergency."
Behind BAYANIHanda: Building Awareness in Youth Against Natural-hazards and DRRM concepts through Interactive Games towards Enhanced Preparedness and Resilience. "The name plays on three Filipino words: bayani, a hero; handa, meaning ready; and bayanihan, the spirit of neighbors helping neighbors." Shared open library for educators/developers. Lead developer: Edward Andrew A. Dionido, Lead Science Research Specialist II, University of the Philippines Resilience Institute. Built with the assistance of AI.
Settings: Sound and voice · Read tips and hints aloud.

## Pillars & games
MIT "Stop Hazards Early" (Prevention and Mitigation) — lead "Spot dangers before they happen and stop them from turning into disasters."
 - Guide the Stream 🏞 — "Clear the silt and open a channel so the creek reaches the sea instead of flooding homes. Inspired by Ang Sapa." 3 puzzles: Clear the creek / Around the barangay / The blocked culvert. Badge Water Warden 💧
 - Hazard Hunt 🗺 — "Pick a place, then spot the things that turn dangerous in an earthquake or a typhoon, and learn how to fix them." Places: At home 🏠 / At school 🏫 / On the street 🏙. Badge Hazard Hunt
PREP "Get Ready" (Preparedness) — "Get your family and your go bag ready before anything happens."
 - Go Bag Packing 3D 🎒 — "Grab the right supplies from the room and pack a smart go bag." Modes: On the table / Messy pile / Search the kitchen. Steps Look/Tap/Pack/Zip. Badge Go Bag
RESP "Act Fast" (Response) — "Learn what to do in the first minutes when a disaster strikes."
 - Fire Extinguisher 3D 🧯 — "Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep. Put out the fire the PASS way." Easy/Difficult. Badge Fire Out
 - Duck, Cover, and Hold 🏗 — "Watch the coach, then copy the moves: drop, cover under the sturdy table, hold on, then walk out." Badge Quake Ready
 - Safe Path 🧭 — "Move tile by tile to the evacuation area, keeping away from coasts, trees, poles, and buildings when a hazard strikes." Levels: Trace the route / Tile escape / High ground. Badge Safe Path
 - Coming soon: Nobody Left Behind 🚪, Stop the Bleed 🩹, Hands-Only CPR 💓 (badges No One Left, Stop Bleed, CPR Hero)
REC "Bounce Back" (Recovery and Rehabilitation) — "After a disaster, help your home and your community bounce back."
 - House Cleanup 🧹 — flood (inside) / typhoon (outside); keep, throw, don't touch; bins bio/recyclable/special. Badge Cleanup Crew
 - CARES: Community and Resilience 🏘 — build community by stream, green+gray protection, simulate. 4 maps. Badge Community Builder
Fire win: "Fire is out!" Time. Safety: "Real life reminder. Only fight a small fire, and only with a clear way out behind you. If the fire is big, spreading, or the room fills with smoke, get out fast, close the door, pull the fire alarm, and call for help."
Result pattern: ★★★ + headline + "New badge earned for your Champion ID!" + Play again / Next / Home.
