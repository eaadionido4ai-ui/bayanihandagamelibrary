5Read at a story stand NEW SCREENStories, poems and letters sit on stands and reading walls in every room. Walk up, tap Read, and listen line by line.
✅ Same app, new wingTANGHALAN reuses the games' 3D engine, Bayani's voice and the players on the device. Every work links back to a game, so the museum feeds the library.
🚪 One connected museumHalls open into each other like one big building. Kids roam freely with their player: art hangs on the walls, and stories and poems wait on story stands.
🔒 Consent comes firstTeachers submit works with a parent's signed consent. Only first name, age and province are shown: no faces, full names or schools. The team checks each work before it goes up.
🌱 Grows with every classNew rooms open as more schools send works, and the featured hall changes with each exhibit. Rooms keep their pillar colors, so kids always know where a work belongs.
